# Design System

## Visual authority

Extend the incumbent Koko Atelier public site: white and pale-slate working surfaces, deep navy brand fields, restrained blue accents, documentary atelier photography and direct sans-serif typography. The private product shifts from persuasion to operation without becoming a generic analytics dashboard.

## Tokens and material

- Brand navy (`brand-900`) anchors headers and primary actions; blue (`brand-600/700`) marks active or actionable states.
- White is the main work surface, `slate-50` the surrounding canvas, and thin slate dividers separate dense operational rows.
- Corners use 12–16px radii for grouped work surfaces; pills are reserved for compact states and language controls.
- Shadows are rare and reserved for raised mobile navigation or focused overlays.

## Typography and spacing

Use the existing system sans stack. Headings are semibold with tight but never excessive tracking. Operational views favor strong labels, tabular money and compact vertical rhythm; public sections keep generous breathing room.

## Interaction

Controls have visible keyboard focus, clear active/disabled states and minimum comfortable touch targets. Mobile navigation stays reachable at the bottom. Overlays interrupt only for protected message previews or focused data entry.

## Responsive behavior

The private demo is designed at 390px first, with single-column task rows and a fixed bottom navigation. At desktop widths it becomes a left navigation rail plus a wider content canvas. No essential action relies on hover.
