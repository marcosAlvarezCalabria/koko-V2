# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Liudmyla Arnautova, a Galway tailor managing predominantly in-person alteration jobs from her phone. Prospective local customers use the public site to understand the service and request a quote.

## Product Purpose

Koko Atelier Galway presents Liudmyla’s craft and demonstrates a lightweight workflow for requests, jobs, customers, prices, payments and customer updates. Success for this phase is a credible, sellable demonstration before investing in a backend.

## Positioning

A personal atelier workflow shaped around the physical handover of garments, fittings, due dates and collection—not a generic sales CRM.

## Operating Context

The tailor receives garments in person, records work and price details on a mobile device, tracks what needs attention, and prepares English WhatsApp updates for customers in Ireland. The private interface can be used in English or Ukrainian.

## Capabilities and Constraints

This phase is a backend-free demo. Data stays in versioned browser localStorage. WhatsApp is preview-only. Google Calendar, payments, multi-user teams and a live WhatsApp inbox are excluded. Public contact details, address and hours remain unconfirmed placeholders.

## Brand Commitments

The confirmed name is Koko Atelier Galway. Public communication is in English. The private interface supports English and Ukrainian. The product must preserve the existing site’s restrained navy, blue and white identity and authentic atelier imagery.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains atelier and garment imagery, an existing public demonstration, and Liudmyla’s approved biography. It contains no confirmed contact details, opening hours, testimonials or live commercial prices; none may be presented as confirmed.

## Product Principles

- Start from the garment handover, not a generic CRM pipeline.
- Surface today’s work, overdue jobs and money still due.
- Keep every demo action visibly safe and reversible.
- Use plain language and one-hand mobile actions.
- Validate the customer need before adding infrastructure.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast and responsive layouts are required. The private workflow must remain complete in both supported languages.
